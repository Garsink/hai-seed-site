How it connects to your business.

How does HAI connect to our information?

You choose the systems and files HAI connects to, including email, documents, ERP and accounting. There is no need to move everything into one company cloud. Start with the sources your first question needs and add more as you go.

Does HAI use its own AI model?

HAI uses established AI models. What it adds is your company’s history, records and systems, connected and kept current, plus shared rules and routines. You can ask about the whole business without explaining it from scratch each time.

Where is our company knowledge stored?

HAI stores the knowledge it builds from your company’s history, documents and connected systems on your company’s own device. You choose which sources it can use.

From your sources to a decision.

One system with three connected parts. Every answer can be traced back through them.

  1. Your sources

    Wherever they live

    Email, chat, documents, meeting notes, your ERP and accounting, recordings and outside data. We plan the connections around your business. What each setup includes

  2. HAI Root

    Your company’s memory

    Links each record to the people, work and decisions around it. It learns from your corrections and stays current on an agreed schedule.

  3. HAI Kernel

    Rules and routines

    Runs the checks you agree, on schedule, within your rules and permissions, and keeps a record of what ran and where it went.

  4. HAI Crown

    Answers and prepared work

    Answers with the sources and gaps visible, flags what changed and prepares work for a person to review.

One real route through it

  1. A customer’s email, last week’s meeting notes, ERP orders and accounting
  2. Root links them to the same customer, supplier and promises
  3. Kernel checks each promise against what the records now say
  4. Crown writes the owner’s brief: the decisions to take, each with a recommendation

Getting started.

Guided self-onboarding, with a dedicated engineer available from day one.

  1. 1Choose one question

    Start with one recurring question or task, the sources it needs and who may use it.

  2. 2Connect and correct

    HAI reads the sources and shows what it understood. You correct it, and it remembers.

  3. 3Grow by role

    Add teams as it proves useful. Agree each team’s sources and access before adding them.

Day to day

Ask a short question in your HAI workspace in the AI app. HAI brings the company history and live records behind it together, so you get to the decision without first gathering the story yourself. Reports and drafts arrive in your company folder for a person to review. Scheduled checks and briefs follow the routes agreed during setup. The first 30 days of use are included.
