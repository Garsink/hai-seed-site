You stay in control.

Agree what HAI can read, prepare and do during setup. People review the work and make the decisions. You can pause HAI’s routines, and each run keeps a record of what happened and where it was delivered.

We already use AI. What does HAI add?

AI can do a lot with a short prompt. Your AI account provides the reasoning. HAI adds accumulated company knowledge, rules and decisions, with sources, access and checks that we configure and maintain.

It uses established AI models. “Your own AI” means a setup built around your company; you do not need to train a new model.

Where is information stored and processed?

Company memory is stored on the deployment machine. Connected AI services process task information under your chosen account and service terms. Local installation does not mean AI processing is entirely offline. We explain accounts, sources and support access before connecting data. Messages to customers or suppliers and system changes are scoped and checked separately.

What can HAI do on its own?

Read approved information, analyse it, run scheduled checks and prepare answers, briefs and work for review. Changes in your systems and messages to customers or suppliers need separately agreed configuration and validation.

Does it work without us asking?

Yes. Agreed routines can check changes, flag unusual activity, prepare reports and follow up on commitments. You decide what runs and which actions need approval.

Inside HAI Seed.

Three connected parts. One company memory, one set of rules and one place to turn knowledge into action.

  • HAI Root

    Company memory

    Connects your records to the people, work and decisions behind them. Learns from your corrections and stays current on an agreed schedule.

  • HAI Kernel

    Rules, routines and checks

    Runs the checks you agree, within your permissions, and keeps a record of what ran and where it went.

  • HAI Crown

    Answers and action

    Answers with sources, flags what changed and prepares work for you to review. Shows where information is missing.

Your sources, wherever they live. Email, chat, documents, meetings, business systems, recordings and outside data. We plan the connections around your business. What each setup includes.

Start with you. Bring in your teams.

Give each team its own access to the same company memory. Scheduled routines keep watch between questions.

An agent is a program that uses AI and tools to do a job. Use HAI directly, or add agents where you need them. They share the same knowledge and rules; corrections feed back into Root.

Let HAI find what to improve.

“Review our company. What should we fix, improve or automate?”

HAI reviews your records against relevant best practices, shows what needs attention and recommends changes. You choose the priorities; HAI helps build the routines or agents. New agents are scoped and agreed in advance.
