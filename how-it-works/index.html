How HAI Seed works.

HAI Seed connects AI to your company’s information and rules. Your team asks in plain language and gets answers from your own records. Between questions, HAI checks what changed and flags what needs attention.

From seed to answers.

Every setup grows the same way: it starts with the owner and spreads across the company.

  1. 1Start with the owner

    Our engineer installs HAI Seed and sets up one workflow around the owner’s questions.

  2. 2Build shared knowledge

    HAI gathers the sources you choose and learns from your team’s examples and corrections.

  3. 3Expand across teams

    Each team gets its own HAI on the same company memory, within the access you set.

What your team gets.

Each kind of help comes from the same company memory.

Answers

Ask in one line. HAI answers from your records and shows the sources behind each point.

Which lost quotes could we win back now?

Heads-ups

Recurring checks compare your records with your priorities and say what changed, why it matters and what to do next.

A key contract renews next week at last year’s prices.

Prepared work

Briefs, proposals, comparisons and meeting follow-ups, drafted for a person to review.

What do buyers ask us most before they order?

What HAI reads.

The sources you choose, through supported connections or agreed exports. Your mail, accounting and other systems stay as they are.

  • Email in Microsoft 365
  • Shared files and documents
  • ERP and accounting exports
  • Meeting notes and decisions
  • Company policies
  • Your corrections and examples

Company memory is stored on the deployment machine. Connected AI services process the information used for each task, under your chosen account and service terms.

Inside the Seed.

One system with three connected parts, included in every setup.

HAI Root

Your company’s memory.

  • Connects mail, files and system exports.
  • Links each record to the people, work and decisions around it.
  • Learns from your team’s examples and corrections, and stays current on an agreed schedule.

HAI Kernel

The operating core.

  • Runs routines and checks on schedule, with shared data-use rules and pause controls.
  • Keeps a record of what ran and where it was delivered.

HAI Crown

Your company’s intelligence.

  • Answers questions with the sources and any gaps visible.
  • Flags risks, anomalies, missed commitments and opportunities.
  • Follows agreed commitments into the next review.
  • Gives each role its own HAI, within the access you set.

Guided self-onboarding.

HAI does much of the work of gathering, organising and connecting your information. A dedicated engineer from HAI Seed guides the setup, from the first call to handover.

  1. 1

    Agree the scope

    Choose one recurring workflow, the sources it needs, who has access and how the result is checked.

  2. 2

    Connect and review

    The engineer connects the sources. Your team checks what HAI understood and corrects it.

  3. 3

    Hand over

    Real cases are tested, two training sessions are held and acceptance is recorded. The first 30 days of use are included.
