How it works↓

HAI Seed is software that runs on your own company computer or on a machine we preconfigure. An AI model does the thinking. HAI gives it your company’s knowledge, your tools and your rules for how work is done.

HAI RootConnect and remember

It learns what your company already knows.

You choose which email, documents, meetings and business systems HAI may read. Root brings them together, keeps them current and links them to the people, work and decisions behind them. Your corrections become part of this shared company memory.

Company memory stays on your HAI Seed computer. Each task uses the local or external AI model chosen for your setup. Data details

Shared company memory

HAI KernelTurn knowledge into work

It plans the steps and runs the work.

For each question, task or scheduled check, Kernel brings the right company records to the AI model, runs the steps through your tools within your permissions and records what happened. Work can continue between conversations.

HAI CrownTalk to it, put it to work

You ask, hand over work and decide.

Ask in ordinary language, hand over work or review what HAI has noticed. You get answers with sources, work done within agreed limits and clear decisions to make. Each team uses the same base with its own access, and confirmed decisions feed back in.

OwnerSalesFinanceMarketingConfirmed decisions return

The base stays as you add new AI models, tools, agents or automations. Each one starts from the knowledge and rules already in place.

Getting started.

We agree your first scope together. Then guided self-onboarding installs HAI and connects your sources, with an engineer to help. First use can come within hours, depending on your data and access.
