ChatGPT or Your Own Company AI: Which Does a Small Company Need?

A plain answer for owners, including the cases where a chat account is enough.

HAI Seed Lab · 24 November 2026 · 3 min read

If the questions your team asks are general, a ChatGPT account is enough and you should not pay for more. A company AI earns its cost when the answers depend on your own customers, promises and numbers, and when you want work to start without someone asking.

Most small companies end up with both. HAI Seed itself runs on AI accounts like these. It adds what they do not have: your company.

When a chat account is enough

Drafting and rewriting text. Translating. Explaining a regulation or a contract clause. Summarising a document you paste in. Thinking a decision through.

For this kind of work the general knowledge of the model does the job. One person, one question, one answer. It costs a monthly fee per person and setup takes minutes.

If that is most of what your team does with AI, you can stop reading here.

Where it stops

Ask the same chat: which customers have gone quiet, and why?

It can answer only from what you type, paste or connect. Someone has to know which records matter, find them and bring them in. With a hard question, that is exactly the part people do not know in advance.

Three jobs stay with you:

  • Bringing the company context. You find, paste or connect what it needs.
  • Keeping it current. You refresh what it sees.
  • Noticing. A chat answers when asked. Nobody asks about the enquiry that never got a reply.

For a small company that is real money. It makes sense when one recurring piece of work, such as a weekly owner brief, is worth more to you than the monthly fee.

A test before you spend anything

Ask your colleagues and your current AI these three questions today:

  1. What should I decide this week?
  2. Which customers have gone quiet, and why?
  3. What did we promise that we have not done?

Note how long each answer takes and what is missing.

If the answers come back fast and complete, you do not need a company AI yet. If they take days and three people, that gap is the work a company AI is built for.
