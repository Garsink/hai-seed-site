What I Mean by Company AI

Not a smarter chat window. A company that keeps what it knows, notices what matters and gets better at its own work.

Deividas Jurkštas, Founder, HAI Seed · 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

I want my company to have an intelligence of its own. That is the first line of the film above, and it is the shortest way I know to say what we are building.

Today most companies run on good people, each holding a piece of the picture. One remembers what was promised. One knows the real costs. One hears what customers keep asking for. Someone has to put it all together and think of the next question. In my company, that someone is usually me.

The foundation comes first

A company AI starts with the company: its history, its systems, its decisions and the reasons behind them. Not a folder you paste into a chat, but a foundation that stays connected and current.

We saw why this matters at Head Aero, our aviation business. A colleague shared a link to 32,424 pages of aircraft records and asked what we could make there. HAI read them, but the most useful findings were not in that folder. In our own email it found an offer for the same engines that we had answered in 2023, and a customer we had quoted for the same part. It proposed a way to pursue it with a partner.

Our own history turned a folder into an opportunity. Not a closed deal, but a reason to make the right call.

Then it starts to look on its own

The second half of the film is imagined, and I want to be clear about that. It shows where we are heading, not what every installation does today.

Customers keep asking for next-day delivery, and the team keeps doing it as a favour. HAI notices the pattern and brings a question nobody asked it: are we giving away something people would pay for? It looks at who asks, how often and what it costs, the way a good analyst would. Our dispatcher adds what no system recorded. One number is missing, so it builds a small tool to measure it and tests it on last month's invoices. We set the limits: three customers, one month, a fixed price. HAI runs the trial within them and reports back with what actually happened.

That is the loop I care about. Notice, investigate, do the work within the limits the owner sets, and learn from the result.

Our own history turned a folder into an opportunity.

Agents are workers, not the point

People ask me whether HAI is an agent. It can create agents and use them, and so can many tools. An agent is a worker for one job. What makes it useful is the foundation underneath: what the company knows, its rules and what it has learned.

Models will get better. Tools and agents will change. People move on. What the company has learned should stay, and keep growing.

What stays with people

We still choose where the company goes. I want to weigh opportunities, not invent every one of them myself. You set the limits. HAI works within them and brings you the decisions that need your judgement.

HAI Seed is how we bring this to other companies. A first installation starts with one useful job and a clear setup. It does not do everything in the film on day one. What it builds stays, and the next job starts from there.

If this is how you think about your own company, I would like to hear from you.
