An overdue invoice is linked to an unresolved delivery complaint. Finance has chased payment, but the customer is still waiting for a resolution. Service and finance need to act together.

Sources
Invoice · customer email · delivery record
Why it matters
Another payment reminder may miss the underlying complaint.
Responsible
Service and finance.
Next step
Service: agree the remedy. Finance: confirm the payment plan with the customer.

Two proposals are waiting on missing information: one needs a technical specification; the other needs a delivery date. Customer emails still contain open questions that the sales notes marked as answered.

Sources
Customer emails · proposal drafts · sales notes
Why it matters
Both proposals depend on questions that remain unresolved.
Responsible
Sales coordinates technical and operations input.
Next step
Technical: confirm the specification. Operations: confirm the date. Sales: revise both proposals.

Friday’s customer delivery is at risk: the order is packed, but the carrier has not confirmed collection. A later dispatch could conflict with the date promised in the customer email.

Sources
Order record · warehouse status · carrier correspondence · customer email
Why it matters
The promised delivery date depends on an unconfirmed collection.
Responsible
Operations confirms collection. Sales reviews the customer update.
Next step
Confirm collection, then review this customer draft: “Your order is packed. We are confirming the delivery date.”
