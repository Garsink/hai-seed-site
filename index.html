Inside HAI Seed

One system. Three connected components.

HAI Seed is built for small and medium-sized businesses. Start with one owner or CEO who wants to understand what is happening across the company, then expand across teams and departments. We install it on a supported company computer or supply a preconfigured machine. Each person uses an AI workspace with the shared knowledge and access their role needs.

HAI Root

Explore HAI Root ↗

Your company’s living memory.

Root connects your company’s knowledge, history and business systems. Through self-onboarding, it gathers, organises and keeps context current, with your team’s guidance. Every setup includes a dedicated engineer to help connect sources, configure access and review what HAI has learned.

HAI Kernel

Explore HAI Kernel ↗

The operating core of HAI Seed.

Kernel is the shared operating foundation of HAI Seed, keeping its capabilities, routines and controls working together. It provides the common core for Root and Crown, from built-in safeguards to system records and updates.

HAI Crown

Explore HAI Crown ↗

Your company’s intelligence, put to work.

Get answers, insights and recommendations in minutes, grounded in your whole company’s context. Crown proactively flags risks, anomalies and opportunities, including things you have not thought to ask about. Scale that intelligence across the company, giving each employee their own HAI with the knowledge and access their role needs.
