The rate stayed the same. The billed quantity increased.

April: 8 units × €75 = €600. May: 12 units × €75 = €900. The €300 difference comes from four additional billed units, not a higher unit price.

Still to check

The service log is missing. We cannot yet tell whether all 12 units were delivered.

Suggested next step

Operations: check the service log. Finance: compare it with the invoice before approving payment.
