The work it does there.

Shared company information supports drafts, checks and authorised follow-ups. Process owners keep approval.

Enquiries and quotes

Reads incoming requests, prices them against stock and history and drafts quotes. Approved offers can go out automatically under set rules.

Purchasing

Tracks requirements, requests supplier offers and records each offer in the system.

Finance

Reads cash, receivables and payables from source systems. Bank data takes precedence over books.

Meetings

Turns recordings into minutes and tracks the follow-ups.

Market

Reads market sources each morning and reports only what changed, with the source.

People

Handles staff questions and paperwork. Sensitive records need an explicit access scope.

Marketing

Finds publishable signals in internal meetings, with names and figures removed.

Knowledge

Keeps mail, chat, meeting and deal records in separate access-controlled logs.

Controls

Checks recipients, screens payment language and keeps the ERP read-only by default.

Keep people in control.

The controls the founder’s company runs on.

Recipient verification

A message must name its recipient in two independent places, and both must match, or it is not sent.

Scope gate

Outbound drafts are classified, every recipient including copies is checked, and the lowest permission in the group applies.

Payment language

Messages containing payment or bank instructions are held unless explicitly released, and every decision is logged.

Figure freshness

A financial figure is re-pulled from its source when the draft is written. If the source is stale, the figure is withheld.

ERP access

Read-only by default. Only approved quoting functions write, to their own records, through one audited path.

Choose what HAI can act on.

Our deployment sends through four channels, each authorised, controlled, logged and stoppable. A new company starts with none and adds a channel only when it approves it.

Internal email

Capped per recipient per day and in total, screened for confidential content, listed in a daily digest.

Customer quotes

Enabled only while an explicit arming file is present. A price floor applies to every quote. Any account can be set to manual only.

Marketplace submissions

Separate stop control.

Public stock listings

Explicit arming, daily cap, separate pause.

One master stop covers all four channels. HAI does not sign documents and does not move money. Stop events report to the responsible person through a separate path.

The September audit record

Technical background (September 2026)

A historical record of the founder’s deployment. These figures and controls are dated examples, not the current product specification or the scope of every HAI Seed setup.

On 2 September 2026, an adversarial audit completed 311 evaluations across four machines and recorded 102 findings. Examples included jobs that reported success while producing incorrect output or reaching nobody. The affected controls were changed during that week.

On 20 September 2026, 20 findings were sampled and manually re-checked against the original logs. 19 held; one was an error in the audit. The checker had reported an alert as missing after reading blocked attempts, although a later entry recorded delivery. That finding was corrected and the checker changed to inspect the final delivery state.

A dated internal record, including the audit’s own correction. It does not establish that every finding was closed or every customer setup has these controls.
