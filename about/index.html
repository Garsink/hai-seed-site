Built by a business owner, inside a working business.

We build HAI Seed so owners and teams can work with AI that understands their company’s history, knowledge and systems.

It began with one owner’s questions.

Deividas Jurkštas, founder of HAI Seed, is an aviation entrepreneur working across parts trading, maintenance and leasing.

HAI began inside his own business, helping connect scattered information to real decisions. That daily use still shapes how we build it, from the owner’s questions to the work of every team.

Built with aviation discipline.

In aviation, a missing record or an inconsistent date matters. That attention to evidence shapes how we build HAI.

We bring the same discipline across the company: connect the context, trace findings to evidence and make uncertainties visible.
