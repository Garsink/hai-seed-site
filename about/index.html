Built with aviation discipline.

In aviation, a missing record or an inconsistent date matters. That attention to evidence shapes how we build HAI.

A component’s back-to-birth (BTB) records document its life history. They can span thousands of pages from different operators, each with its own formats and inconsistencies. HAI brings them together into one coherent history, with gaps and conflicting information highlighted for engineering review.

In the founder’s own use, HAI has prepared this history in around two hours, where the manual work previously took an engineer several days per component. The engineer verifies the findings and makes the final assessment.

We bring the same discipline across the company: connect the context, trace findings to evidence and make uncertainties visible. From finance and operations to sales and people, your team gets a clearer basis for decisions.
