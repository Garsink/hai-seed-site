Website privacy.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

UAB AviaVest is responsible for personal information collected through this website and its enquiries. Company code: 305188331. Registered address: Švitrigailos g. 11M, LT-03228 Vilnius, Lithuania.

Visiting this website.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Demo requests are processed through OpenAI Sites using Cloudflare infrastructure. These services process technical information to deliver and protect the website and enquiry form. We have not added advertising trackers or custom analytics. A temporary browser setting remembers whether the opening animation has played. Our interest is to operate a reliable, secure website.

Contacting us.

We use the contact details and message you provide to answer your enquiry, discuss your requirements and prepare a proposal. Demo requests submitted through the form are stored in a private database accessible to the site owner. Providing information is voluntary, but we need a way to reply.

For business enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding and managing business relationships. Where you personally request steps towards a contract with us, we rely on that pre-contractual purpose. An enquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Services we use.

Email is stored with Forward Email, copied to the founder’s Gmail account and accessed through Outlook. Connected AI services may help organise correspondence and prepare replies, with human review.

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area, including the US. Their published transfer arrangements are available from Forward Email, Google and OpenAI. Hosting provider information is available from GitHub and Cloudflare. Contact us for details of the providers and safeguards relevant to your information.

How long we keep it.

We delete or anonymise unsuccessful enquiries within 12 months of the last meaningful exchange. Records needed for a customer relationship, legal obligations or a specific legal claim follow the relevant retention requirements. Service providers retain operational records under their own published policies.

Your rights.

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to exercise applicable rights to restrict, object to or transfer your information. We normally respond within one month. You may complain to Lithuania’s State Data Protection Inspectorate or another competent supervisory authority.

Customer HAI deployments have separate data arrangements agreed during setup.
