Finance

Overdue receivables look better this week. Why?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Receivables ledger
  • Its change history
  • Bank records

What it found

A payment due date had been moved. The debt was still open and no payment had arrived. Separately, a cash-in total included customer advances.

A clearer picture of actual collections and a specific payment-terms question for the management meeting.

Operations

Which suppliers are quietly costing us customers?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Orders
  • Receipts
  • Suppliers’ offers
  • Lost quotes

What it found

Four suppliers that quote days were delivering in weeks, and a quarter of the quote lines lost or declined for lead time had a faster offer already sitting in the company’s own files.

Proposed

Record each supplier’s promised date on the order and measure them against it, then change who gets the first call. The head of purchasing decides.

Finance

What really made money last month?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Ledger
  • Every invoice line
  • Bill entry dates

What it found

The first read of the month was provisional: a cost from an earlier sale landed a week after month end, and the biggest sale earned its fee in the following month.

Proposed

A hard date after which nothing moves into a closed month, and a decision on what to set aside. The CFO decides the treatment; the owner the provision.

Operations

Where do our orders lose days between the customer’s yes and the aircraft?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Orders
  • Warehouse mailbox
  • Forwarders’ pickups

What it found

On the orders that could be traced the goods were ready in a day or two; most of the lost week sat in the hand-offs around the paperwork, where the system’s clock does not run.

Proposed

Issue the paperwork the day it is requested and tell the forwarder within twenty-four hours. The head of logistics decides.

How these answers were made

Real work, anonymised: people as roles, companies unnamed, no money figures, counts rounded. Sources were connected with the owners’ consent. Times from the run logs cover the analysis; the review passes came after. Results depend on the sources connected and the workflow configured.
