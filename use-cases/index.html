What owners ask HAI.

Real questions from the founder’s company and an early customer. The owner’s questions first, then what each team asks. Each answer joins records the company already had and ends with a decision for a person to take.

Pick a team.

Open a question to see what HAI read, what it found and who decides.

Owner

What did we promise each other, what moved since, and what should I decide this week?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Meeting record
  • Mail
  • Chat
  • Ledgers

What it found

A customer could no longer buy while three tasks still chased the old plan, and a deal already won depended on a supplier the company still owed.

Proposed

Two decisions, each with a recommendation. The owner decides.

HAI read

  • Technical revisions

Finance

Overdue receivables look better this week. Why?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Receivables ledger
  • Its change history
  • Bank records
  • Earlier meeting commitments

What it found

A payment due date had been moved. The debt was still open and no payment had arrived. Separately, a cash-in total included customer advances.

What the owner got

A clearer picture of actual collections and a specific payment-terms question for the management meeting.

The founder’s HAI system.

Finance

What really made money last month?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Ledger
  • Every invoice line
  • Bill entry dates

What it found

The first read of the month was provisional: a cost from an earlier sale landed a week after month end, and the biggest sale earned its fee in the following month.

Proposed

A hard date after which nothing moves into a closed month, and a decision on what to set aside. The CFO decides the treatment; the owner the provision.

The founder’s company, one Sunday morning.

Finance

Why has this recurring service cost gone up?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Declared quantities
  • Cost records
  • Earlier correspondence with the provider

What it found

According to the owner, the increase came from higher declared quantities, not a price rise. HAI also brought back earlier correspondence about reviewing alternative providers.

The cause of the change and its history in one place, giving the owner a concrete basis for the next conversation.

Sales

Why are we losing deals, and what one change would win the most back?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Lost quotes
  • Customers’ emails

What it found

Most quotes marked lost had not been lost to a customer; a nightly job had expired them. In the four largest losses where the customer had written back, the reason on file matched the customer’s words in none.

Proposed

No priced quote is closed as lost without an outcome read from the customer’s thread and confirmed by a person. The head of sales decides.

The founder’s company, one Sunday morning.

Marketing

Where is demand for what we sell in the next two years, and whom have we never spoken to?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Fleet forecast
  • Quote history
  • Trade show lists
  • Page invitations

What it found

Many of the operators with work due had no two-way contact on record, and the largest of them was in the region of the trade show the team was flying to that week.

Proposed

Ten names, ten owners, one quarter. The CMO and the head of sales decide, one name each.

The founder’s company, one Sunday morning.

Operations

Which suppliers are quietly costing us customers?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Orders
  • Receipts
  • Suppliers’ offers
  • Lost quotes

What it found

Four suppliers that quote days were delivering in weeks, and a quarter of the quote lines lost or declined for lead time had a faster offer already sitting in the company’s own files.

Proposed

Record each supplier’s promised date on the order and measure them against it, then change who gets the first call. The head of purchasing decides.

The founder’s company, one Sunday morning.

Operations

Where do our orders lose days between the customer’s yes and the aircraft?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Orders
  • Warehouse mailbox
  • Forwarders’ pickups

What it found

On the orders that could be traced the goods were ready in a day or two; most of the lost week sat in the hand-offs around the paperwork, where the system’s clock does not run.

Proposed

Issue the paperwork the day it is requested and tell the forwarder within twenty-four hours. The head of logistics decides.

The founder’s company, one Sunday morning.

Operations

What will our customers and the auditor find before we do?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • Supplier approvals
  • Orders
  • Returns
  • Two years of supplier scorecards

What it found

HAI listed what the audit would sample first: the approvals due for renewal before it, the receiving records with nothing written at inspection, and one supplier whose rating, approval and open return had never been read together.

Proposed

A sprint to renew approvals and three corrective actions. The quality manager decides.

The founder’s company, one Sunday morning.

People

Who do we owe a decision to in the next 30 days, and who is at risk of leaving?

Read the answerClose

HAI read

  • People register
  • HR calendar
  • Meeting record
  • Owner’s question queue
  • Mail volume per person

What it found
