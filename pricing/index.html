Pricing.

Start with one useful workflow, then grow across your company. Prices exclude VAT; your quote depends on sources, data volume, workflows, users and support.

Setup and monthly price

One-time setup

From €3,000

A working setup through guided self-onboarding, with a dedicated engineer from day one.

  • A dedicated engineer from HAI Seed, from configuration to handover
  • One installation and one recurring workflow that reads, checks and reports, such as a weekly owner brief
  • Up to three source sets, such as selected email, documents or a system export
  • One main user, plus a reviewer who checks results
  • Two training sessions, a practical guide and acceptance checks

Licence and maintenance

From €490 / month

Keeps the setup current as your company changes.

  • A licence for one installation and supported product updates
  • Up to two hours of support each month, including a 30-minute review
  • Source checks, small knowledge or rule changes and everyday help
  • Defect fixes within the agreed scope, without using your hours

Additional users

From €99 / user / month

and from €500 setup per user

Give more people their own HAI workspace, connected to your company’s shared knowledge and limited to what their role may see. Each user works with their own AI account.

  • Setup on a supported computer, access chosen from the sources already connected, a check that restricted information stays out of reach, and a practical handover
  • Licensed use, supported updates and upkeep of the user’s access, with help from your plan’s shared support hours

A reviewer who only checks results needs no extra user. Limits inside a source (for example, only some of its records), new sources or workflows and dedicated installations are quoted. AI accounts and hardware are paid separately.

The first 30 days of use are included from acceptance. Paid use starts only if you choose to continue.

Questions before you start.

How is the work accepted?

We check five normal cases and two cases with missing or conflicting data, repeat the workflow the next day with a correction, and review sources and access. Acceptance and handover are recorded; silence is not acceptance. Any correction and re-check arrangements are agreed in your proposal. If the agreed result still does not meet the acceptance criteria after those steps, the second 50% is not payable.

What happens if we stop?

Your licence and maintenance end on the agreed expiry terms. You keep any hardware you bought and your company documents, knowledge and rules, and the exit terms include exporting your information. Cancelling does not give a right to keep using HAI Seed indefinitely.

Is a monthly price change possible?

Your agreed monthly price is held for 12 months. Any later price change is notified in advance, with the option not to continue.

What do you need from our team?

A sponsor sets priorities. Process owners provide examples, explain the work and review results. Authorised administrators grant agreed access. Together, we identify restricted information and decisions that stay with people, and plan your team’s time before starting.

Our computer or a preconfigured machine?

We can install HAI on your supported computer or supply a preconfigured machine. Company sign-in, access and acceptance are completed with your team. Hardware is purchased separately; your proposal specifies equipment, support and handover.

Where is information stored and processed?

Company memory is stored on the deployment machine. Connected AI services process task information under your chosen account and service terms. We explain accounts, sources and support access before connecting data. Messages to customers or suppliers and system changes are scoped and checked separately.

How long does deployment take?

Timing depends on sources, access and your team’s availability. We agree the schedule after reviewing these; IT approvals or source preparation may affect it.

What if our information is incomplete or outdated?

We check access, freshness and gaps, using supported connections, approved folders or repeatable exports. We test missing and conflicting data with your reviewer before handover; we agree how updates and gaps are checked during use.

We already use AI. What does HAI add?

AI can do a lot with a short prompt. Your AI account provides the reasoning. HAI adds accumulated company knowledge, rules and decisions, with sources, access and checks that we configure and maintain.

Who counts as an additional user?

A named person with their own HAI workspace enabled in a given month, besides the main user. One user is one person, never shared. A reviewer who only checks results is not counted. Users can be removed on the plan’s usual 30 days’ notice.

Does everyone need a dedicated machine?

No. Start on one supported computer. More people can get their own HAI workspace as you grow. Dedicated machines or separate installations are scoped for workload and access separation.

What can HAI Seed do at the start?

Read approved information, analyse it, run scheduled checks and prepare answers, briefs and work for review. Changes in your systems and messages to customers or suppliers need separately agreed configuration and validation. How daily use works

Will HAI replace our existing systems?

Your mail, accounting, CRM and operational systems stay in place as the records to check. HAI uses supported connections or agreed exports.

How do you keep access appropriate?

We agree source and role permissions, with separate scopes for restricted material, then check access and workflows. Local installation does not mean AI processing is entirely offline.

Have a first question in mind?

You get a written scope, price and next step before anything is installed.
