HAI Root

Build a living memory of your company.

Your company’s knowledge lives across systems, documents, conversations and people. HAI Root brings it together, connecting today’s activity with the history, decisions and rules behind it.

Root is the company memory layer of HAI Seed, so your people can work with shared context without rebuilding it for every question.

Self-onboarding,
with your guidance.

Root does much of the work of gathering, organising and connecting information. You give it access and direction, then teach it through conversation, examples and corrections.

  1. Connect

    Choose your starting sources and permissions, using direct connections or agreed exports.

  2. Learn

    Root organises the information and connects relevant context. Your team reviews what it has understood.

  3. Keep current

    Refresh connected sources on an agreed schedule and add new knowledge as the business changes.

A dedicated engineer for every setup.

Your named technical contact helps connect sources, configure the setup and review it with your team, from the first connection through deployment.

From scattered information to company context.

A system record shows what changed. A meeting explains what was agreed. Company history adds the background. Root connects the pieces around the same work, people and decisions.

Root builds the context. Crown puts it to work.

HAI Crown uses this foundation to answer questions, prepare work and surface insights across the business. Owners and teams work from shared knowledge and rules, within the access you define.

HAI Kernel provides the shared operating core, coordinating capabilities, routines and safeguards. Start with the sources that matter most, then expand context and roles as your needs grow.
