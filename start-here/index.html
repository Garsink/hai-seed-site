A clear starting scope.

Starting prices cover the scope below. Your quote depends on sources, data volume and history, workflows, roles, installations and support.

One-time setup · excluding VAT

From €3,000

A working HAI Seed setup, with a dedicated engineer to guide configuration and handover.

What’s included +
  • One installation, one recurring workflow and up to three agreed source sets, such as selected email, documents or a system export.
  • One main user and a reviewer. Separate reviewer access is not included unless agreed.
  • Initial company knowledge and working rules, built through guided self-onboarding.
  • Two training sessions, a practical guide, acceptance checks and handover.

Use your supported computer or buy a preconfigured machine separately. Supported sources, data volume, history and acceptance criteria are agreed in your proposal.

Monthly licence and maintenance · excluding VAT

From €490 / month

Keep your agreed setup supported as your company’s information and working rules evolve.

What’s included +
  • A licence for one installation and supported product updates.
  • Up to two hours total of support and maintenance each month, including a 30-minute review.
  • Within those hours: source connection and update checks, small knowledge or rule changes and everyday help.
  • Defect fixes within the agreed, delivered scope are free and do not use your support hours.

Unused hours do not carry over. Your proposal names the support contact and arrangements; no 24/7 service is included.

The first 30 days of use and maintenance are included from acceptance. Paid use starts only if you choose to continue.

What costs extra+

A wider setup
Additional roles or installations, substantial new sources or workflows, custom integrations and large-scale data preparation.
Additional work & actions
Work beyond the support allowance, outgoing messages and changes to connected systems. Connected actions need their own agreed scope and checks.

We review compatible services and the expected total cost before starting. Additional work is quoted and agreed in advance; there are no automatic charges for extra hours.

When do we pay, and how is the work accepted?

Pay 50% of the setup fee when agreed access is ready and work starts, and 50% after acceptance. We check five normal cases and two cases with missing or conflicting data, repeat the workflow the next day with a correction, and review sources and access. Acceptance and handover are recorded; silence is not acceptance. Any correction and re-check arrangements are agreed in your proposal. If the agreed result still does not meet the acceptance criteria after those steps, the second 50% is not payable.

Is there an annual commitment?

No. Your agreed monthly price is held for 12 months, without a commitment to buy a full year. After choosing paid continuation, you can cancel with 30 days’ notice. Any later price change is notified in advance, with the option not to continue.

What happens if we stop?

Your licence and maintenance end on the agreed expiry terms. You keep any hardware you bought and your company documents, knowledge and rules, and the exit terms include exporting your information. Cancelling does not give a right to keep using HAI Seed indefinitely.

What we need from your team+

A sponsor sets priorities. Process owners provide examples, explain the work and review results. Authorised administrators grant agreed access. Together, we identify restricted information and decisions that stay with people, and plan your team’s time before starting.

Your computer or a preconfigured machine+

We can install HAI on your supported computer or supply a preconfigured machine. Company sign-in, access and acceptance are completed with your team. Hardware is purchased separately; your proposal specifies equipment, support and handover.

Where information is stored and processed+

Company memory is stored on the deployment machine. Connected AI services process task information under your chosen account and service terms. We explain accounts, sources and support access before connecting data. Outgoing actions and system changes are scoped and checked separately.

A few practical questions.

How long does deployment take?

Timing depends on sources, access and your team’s availability. We agree the schedule after reviewing these; IT approvals or source preparation may affect it.

How do we use HAI day to day?

Ask in your HAI workspace in the AI app; review answers there and reports or drafts in your company folder. Add sources or confirm corrections for later tasks. Scheduled checks, briefs and delivery routes are agreed during setup.

What if our information is incomplete or outdated?

We check access, freshness and gaps, using supported connections, approved folders or repeatable exports. We test missing and conflicting data with your reviewer before handover; we agree how updates and gaps are checked during use.

We already use AI. What does HAI add?

AI can do a lot with a short prompt. Your AI account provides the reasoning. HAI adds accumulated company knowledge, rules and decisions, with sources, access and checks that we configure and maintain.

Does everyone need a dedicated machine?

No. Start on one supported computer. Additional role-based environments or machines are scoped for workload and access separation.

What can HAI Seed do at the start?

Read approved information, analyse it and prepare answers and work for review. System changes, outgoing messages and recurring connected actions require separately agreed configuration and validation.

Will HAI replace our existing systems?

Your mail, accounting, CRM and operational systems stay in place as the records to check. HAI uses supported connections or agreed exports.

How do you keep access appropriate?

We agree source and role permissions, with separate scopes for restricted material, then check access and workflows. Local installation does not mean AI processing is entirely offline.
