A capable assistant you talk to. It works from what you type, paste or connect.

CopilotsAI inside your office tools

AI built into the office tools you already use, for email, documents and meetings. It works from those tools, and from other systems your IT connects to it.

AI agentsBought and set up one by one

A program that uses AI and tools to do one job, such as chasing invoices or sorting enquiries. HAI is not an agent, but it can create agents in a few minutes and gives them a brain: your company’s memory and rules.
