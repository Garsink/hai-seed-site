HAI Crown

Your company’s intelligence, put to work.

Crown is the intelligence layer of HAI Seed. See what is happening, understand what matters and know where to act. It connects current activity with your company’s history, knowledge and rules to deliver answers, insights and recommendations in minutes.

For owners, that means a clearer view of the whole business. For teams, it means the context and support to move work forward.

Know what needs your attention. Even before you ask.

Crown uses recurring checks to stay active between conversations, checking connected information against your priorities and agreed rules. It surfaces inconsistencies, anomalies, emerging risks, missed commitments and opportunities you might not think to ask about.

It explains why they matter and recommends what to do next, combining your company’s context with established business practices from around the world.

  1. What changed
  2. Why it matters
  3. What to do next

Ask in plain language. Get a way forward.

  • “What needs my attention this week, and why?”
  • “Where are sales getting stuck?”
  • “What is putting pressure on our margins?”
  • “Which commitments are at risk, and who owns the next step?”
  • “What would you improve first?”

Get relevant facts, explanations, options and recommended next steps, with sources and gaps visible for review.

One company intelligence. A HAI for every employee.

Give each employee their own HAI, connected to the central company intelligence through dedicated, permissioned access. Each works with the knowledge and tools their role needs, building on the shared company memory maintained by Root.

Grow your company’s capacity, person by person. Help people handle broader work, reduce repeated effort and turn collective knowledge into a competitive advantage.

Carry decisions through.

Turn recommendations into owned actions. Crown follows agreed commitments, checks progress against connected records and brings unfinished work into the next review.

Your people set responsibilities and approval points. HAI Kernel provides the shared operating core, coordinating capabilities, routines and safeguards.
