HAI Kernel

The operating core of HAI Seed.

HAI Kernel is the shared operating foundation of HAI Seed, keeping its capabilities, routines and controls working together.

Included in every HAI Seed setup, alongside Root and Crown.

One system.
A shared foundation.

Root builds and keeps your company’s memory. Crown uses it to answer questions, surface insights and prepare work. Kernel provides the operating core they share.

What keeps HAI working together.

  • Connected capabilities

    Keeps a record of connected sources, capabilities, agents and routines, and coordinates how HAI’s modules run.

  • Shared safeguards

    Provides common data-use rules, privacy checks and pause controls for the system.

  • Visible operation

    Tracks system status and activity, with records of what ran and delivery through the owner’s configured channels.

  • Managed evolution

    Provides a shared core and controlled update process as your HAI setup grows.

Part of every HAI Seed.

Root, Kernel and Crown come together in one HAI Seed setup, configured and reviewed with your team around your company’s work.
